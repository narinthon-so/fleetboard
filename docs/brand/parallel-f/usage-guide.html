PARALLEL F / USAGE GUIDE

Independent work streams resolve into one clear F.

01 / Choose the right file

Horizontal is the primary logo. Use stacked in narrow layouts, wordmark where the name leads, and symbol for compact placements.

Use SVG on screens and in layouts. Transparent PNGs are supplied at 512/1024 px; horizontal PNGs at 1024/2048 px. White files have transparent backgrounds.

Use web/favicon.* for browser tabs and the supplied full-bleed PNGs for platform icons.

02 / Clear space

Keep x clear on every side.
x = the vertical stem width (40 units on the 256-unit master). Existing master padding counts toward this zone.

For the small cut, x = 48 units. Browser favicon and app tiles use their supplied fixed padding; do not add padding inside those files.

04 / Backgrounds

Use the colour or ink version on white or Paper. Use the white version on Ember, Ink, and other dark surfaces.

Ember on white: 5.18:1; Ember on Paper: 4.67:1; Ink on Paper: 14.04:1; white on Ink: 15.57:1.

Keep busy imagery outside the clear space or put the logo on a solid approved tile. Use white rather than Ember for small marks on Ink.

06 / Typography

The wordmark uses DejaVu Sans Mono Bold with custom optical spacing and outlined letters. Use the supplied paths; typing the name does not reproduce its spacing.

For supporting copy, use the system sans-serif stack. Use monospace for code and technical labels. Font licence: source/font-license.txt.

07 / Preserve the mark

  • Scale proportionally and keep the 45° terminals.
  • Use the supplied lightened white symbol on dark surfaces.
  • Keep the two pieces and their channel at the fixed proportions.
  • Keep colours flat; avoid outlines, shadows, gradients, rotation, or rearranging lockups.
